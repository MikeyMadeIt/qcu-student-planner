    Privacy Policy

    1.1Introduction and Scope

    This Privacy Policy describes how the QCU Student Planner collects, uses, processes, stores, and protects your personal information. This policy applies to all users accessing and using the platform.

    1.2Project Ownership and Non-Affiliation

    The QCU Student Planner is an independent academic project developed by IM101 Group 5, student developers from SBCS 2A at Quezon City University (QCU). We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Quezon City University (QCU).

    1.3Categories of Personal Information Collected

    To provide, personalize, and secure the QCU Student Planner, we collect only the personal and academic information for its core features, such as academic tracking, AI assisted study, and secure account management. This information is provided by you. The types of information we collect include the following:

    1.3.1Account Registration and Authentication Information

    The Platform uses Google OAuth as its only sign-in and sign-up method (managed through Supabase Auth). When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account’s email address and basic profile info, and we assign you a user role (student).

    1.3.2Academic and Profile Information

    Your full name, academic program, campus, and year level (collected at account registration), along with subjects, class schedules, tasks, assignments, grades, attendance records, and personal calendar events you create while using the Platform.

    1.3.3User Generated Content and Records

    Personal notes, flashcards, quizzes, and other study materials you create and save within the platform, which are stored in our systems. Separately, files you attach to AI-powered features (e.g., PDF, JPG, or PNG files up to 8MB) are transmitted to our AI processing backend and a third-party AI provider solely to generate the requested output, and are not retained as stored files after processing.

    1.3.4AI Prompts and Generated Content

    Text prompts, topics, and the names of any files you submit to the AI Quiz Maker, AI Flashcard Maker, and AI Study Assistant; the AI-generated outputs produced from them (quizzes, flashcard decks, and assistant responses); and your quiz attempt results (scores and answers). Submitted file content is transmitted to our AI processing backend to generate output and is not retained after processing — only the file name is kept as part of the record.

    1.3.5Technical Information and System Logs

    Session and refresh tokens managed by our authentication provider to keep you signed in. Our infrastructure provider (Supabase) and hosting provider may also log standard technical data such as IP addresses and browser/device information at the network level for security and abuse-prevention purposes; this is not stored within the application’s own database.

    1.4Purpose and Legal Basis of Data Processing

    Your data is processed based on your consent and the necessity to provide the platform’s core functionalities. Processing purposes include:

    • Facilitating account creation and manual administrator approval.
    • Organizing and displaying your academic schedules, tasks, and grades.
    • Generating study materials via Google Gemini AI integrations.
    • Maintaining system security and isolating your data using Row Level Security (RLS).

    1.5Data Storage and Third-Party Service Providers

    Data is securely stored using Supabase (PostgreSQL databases and Supabase Storage). Supabase Storage is used to host institution-provided reference files (such as curriculum documents) — not user-uploaded personal files, which are not stored in this bucket. The Platform is hosted on Vercel. We utilize the Google Gemini API to process AI requests via Supabase Edge Functions. These third-party providers act as data processors and have their own privacy policies.

    1.6Data Security and Access Restrictions

    We implement security measures to protect your data. Personal data stored in our database is protected using PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies, which are designed to ensure users can only access and modify their own records. Authorized administrators may access limited account data (such as verification status and support requests) as necessary to operate the Platform. We periodically audit these access controls and apply fixes as needed.

    1.7User Data Isolation

    The QCU Student Planner enforces per-user data isolation at the database level through PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) and Supabase Authentication. Each academic record (tasks, grades, attendance, schedule, calendar and notes) is linked to a unique user ID, and RLS policies are designed to prevent one account from accessing another’s data. This means your academic information can only be accessed through your own authenticated session, except where explicitly noted (see Section 1.6 regarding administrator access to account status and support data).

    1.8Data Sharing and Disclosure

    We do not sell your personal information. Data is only shared with our integrated service providers (Supabase, Vercel, Google Gemini) strictly for the purpose of operating the Platform, or if required by Philippine law.

    1.9Data Retention and Deletion

    Your academic data (tasks, grades, attendance, schedule, calendar entries, and notes) is retained for as long as your account remains active on the Platform. We do not currently run an automatic deletion or data-purge schedule. If you wish to have your account and associated data deleted, you may request this at any time by contacting us at qcustudentplanner.team@gmail.com; such requests are reviewed and processed manually by a Platform administrator, and your data will be permanently removed once the request is verified and completed.

    1.10User Rights Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act

    Under Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), you have the right to:

    • Be informed about how your data is processed.
    • Access your personal data.
    • Object to processing.
    • Erase or block your data.
    • Rectify inaccurate data.
    • Data portability.

    To exercise these rights, or to submit a privacy request, please contact us at qcustudentplanner.team@gmail.com.

    1.11Data Breach and Security Incident Handling

    In the event of a data breach compromising personal information, we will notify affected users and the National Privacy Commission (NPC) in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012. Report security incidents to qcustudentplanner.team@gmail.com

    1.12Policy Updates and Contact Information

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revised version will be posted on this page, and your continued use of the platform following such updates constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

    Acceptable Use Policy

    3.1Permitted Use of the Platform

    The Platform is intended strictly for personal academic organization, scheduling, record tracking, and study assistance. You may use its features to manage your academic activities, organize your notes and study materials, and access educational tools provided by the QCU Student Planner.

    3.2Prohibited Behaviors

    You are strictly prohibited from engaging in any activity that may compromise the security, integrity, or proper operation of the Platform. This includes the following:

    3.2.1Unauthorized Account Access

    Logging into an account that does not belong to you, or allowing another person to use your Google account to access the Platform on your behalf.

    3.2.2Attempting to Access Another User’s Records

    Attempting to bypass Row Level Security (RLS) policies or exploit system vulnerabilities to access another user’s data.

    3.2.3Database and Security Abuse

    Sending automated, excessive, or malicious requests to the Supabase PostgreSQL database.

    3.2.4Uploading Malicious or Inappropriate Files

    Uploading viruses, malware, or inappropriate or illegal files through the Platform’s AI-powered features (e.g., as attachments to the AI Quiz Maker, AI Flashcard Maker, or AI Study Assistant).

    3.2.5Misuse of AI Features

    Submitting prompts that violate the Google Gemini API terms, including requests to generate harmful, illegal, or explicit content.

    3.2.6Account Sharing and Impersonation

    Sharing accounts or impersonating administrators, faculty members, or other students.

    3.3Reporting Violations

    If you observe or suspect a violation of this Acceptable Use Policy report it immediately to qcustudentplanner.team@gmail.com. Include relevant details to help the project maintainers investigate and address the issue promptly.

    3.4Consequences of Policy Violations

    Violations may result in enforcement actions at the discretion of the platform administrators, including account suspension, permanent termination, and removal of content. Severe or unlawful activities, such as unauthorized system access, database abuse, or security interference, may be reported to the appropriate authorities under applicable Philippine laws, including Republic Act No. 10175 (Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012).

    AI Features Disclaimer

    4.1Scope of AI Features

    The Platform provides the following AI features to support your academic organization, study preparation, and learning needs:

    4.1.1AI Quiz Maker

    The Platform provides an AI Quiz Maker designed to generate practice questions from your study materials.

    4.1.2AI Flashcard Maker

    The Platform includes an AI Flashcard Maker that generates flashcards from a topic you enter, notes you type in, or a file you attach, extracting key concepts and definitions for review.

    4.1.3AI Study Assistant

    An AI Study Assistant is available to help summarize complex topics, answer study related queries, and provide contextual academic support.

    4.1.4AI-Generated Answers and Explanations

    The answers, summaries, and explanations produced by these features are generated using probabilistic language models rather than a fixed database of facts.

    4.2Accuracy and Reliability Limitations

    AI generated answers and explanations are probabilistic and may contain factual errors or outdated information. AI content should not be treated as official academic information.

    4.3User Responsibility to Verify Generated Content

    Users are solely responsible for independently verifying the accuracy of any generated quizzes, flashcards, or study materials before using them for academic preparation.

    4.4Uploaded File and Prompt Processing

    When using these tools, your text prompts and uploaded files may be transmitted externally for processing. Prompts are limited to 12,000 characters, and attached files must be a PDF, JPG, or PNG up to 8MB. Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information into the AI system.

    4.5Third-Party AI Provider Disclosure

    The platform’s AI features are powered by the Google Gemini API and processed through Supabase Edge Functions. By using these features, you acknowledge that your inputs may be handled by this third-party provider.

    4.6AI Content Should Not Be Treated as Official Academic Information

    All AI generated outputs are intended only as tools for study aids and do not serve as official, recognized, or authoritative academic course material.

    4.7No Guarantee of Uninterrupted AI Availability

    AI features are provided on an “as is” basis. Their availability depends on third-party API uptime, server stability, and usage limits, and access may be interrupted, delayed, or restricted without notice.

    Cookie and Data Storage Notice

    5.1Browser Storage and Local Storage

    We utilize your browser's local storage capabilities rather than traditional cookies to manage your active state on the Platform.

    5.2Authentication Tokens and Session Information

    Secure authentication tokens issued by Supabase Auth are saved in your browser's local storage to reliably maintain your active login session as you navigate the system.

    5.3User Preferences and Application Settings

    Your personal user preferences and application settings, such as your chosen interface layout or dark/light theme, are saved locally on your device.

    5.4Essential Storage Required for System Functionality

    All local storage utilized by the Platform is strictly essential for core system functionality; we do not store unnecessary or non-essential data on your device.

    5.5Third-Party Cookies or Tracking Technologies, If Applicable

    The Platform does not use any third-party cookies, tracking pixels, or marketing technologies to monitor your activity across other websites.

    5.6Data Stored in the Database vs. Data Stored in the Browser

    Your academic records (subjects, schedules, tasks, grades, attendance, notes, and calendar events) are stored in the Supabase PostgreSQL database as the source of truth, but the Platform also keeps a local working copy of this data in your browser’s local storage for offline access and performance, which is kept in sync with the database. Session tokens and UI preferences are stored in local storage only and are never written to the database.

    5.7Clearing Browser Data

    If you clear your browser’s cache or local storage, you will be logged out and any locally cached academic data and UI preferences on that device will be cleared. Data that has already been synced to the database will not be lost and will reload once you sign in again; however, any changes made while offline that had not yet synced may be lost.
