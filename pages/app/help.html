Answers to common questions about the planner.

Frequently asked questions

Your schedule, tasks, grades, attendance and notes are stored locally in this browser. Your account (sign-in email and profile) is managed separately through your QCU Planner account.

Use the Add Task button on the Tasks page to create a task. To back up or move your data, go to Settings → Backup & Export and choose Export All Data.

Open your profile menu (top right, or the bottom of the sidebar on mobile) and pick Light, Dark or AMOLED. Your choice is remembered on this device.

Use the Report page to send details to the QCU Planner admins.
Still need help?

Contact your program's administrator through the university, or use Report a problem to send a message that reaches the planner's admins.
