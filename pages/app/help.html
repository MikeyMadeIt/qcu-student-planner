Getting started

New to the planner? These four steps get you set up.

  1. Sign in with Google. Use the Google account you registered with. New accounts wait for an administrator to approve them before the planner opens.
  2. Complete your profile. If asked, add your course, year level and school. You can update them later in Settings.
  3. Set up your semester and subjects. Add your subjects and class times in Schedule. Each semester keeps its own subjects, attendance, grades, tasks and notes.
  4. Start tracking. Add tasks, log attendance, record grades and write notes. Your dashboard pulls it all together.

Frequently asked questions

Getting started

What is QCU Student Planner?

QCU Student Planner is a web app that helps students organize their academic life in one place: class schedule, tasks and deadlines, grades and GWA, attendance, notes, and AI-powered study tools. It is an independent student-developed project, not an official university system (see the disclaimer).

Who can use the application?

Students who register with a Google account in the lastname.firstname.middlename@gmail.com format and whose account has been approved by an administrator. Other Google accounts are not accepted.

How do I access my account?

Open the log-in page and choose Continue with Google, then pick your registered account. Once you are signed in you can also install the planner on your device from Settings → App Installation, if your browser supports it.

How does the account approval process work?
  1. You register and your details (including your program, campus and year level) are recorded.
  2. Your account stays pending while an administrator reviews it.
  3. If it is approved, you can log in and use the planner.
  4. If it is not approved, the log-in page tells you, and shows the reason when the administrator gave one.

Approval is always decided by an administrator. The Check status button on the pending page tells you where your account stands.

Account & sign-in

How do I sign in using Google?

On the log-in page, select Continue with Google and choose the Google account registered under your lastname.firstname.middlename@gmail.com address. If Google shows several accounts, make sure you pick that one.

Why is my account still pending approval?

An administrator has to review every new account, and there is no fixed review time. In the meantime you can:

  • Select Check status on the pending page.
  • Make sure your course, year level and school were filled in, because administrators use them to review your account.
  • If it has been a long time, ask through the temporary support channels.
What happens if my account is rejected or suspended?

The log-in page shows a message instead of opening the planner:

  • Rejected: "Your registration was not approved", with the reason if the administrator provided one.
  • Suspended: "Your account has been suspended. Contact an administrator for help."

You cannot use the planner while an account is in either state. To ask about it, use the temporary support channels.

What should I do if I cannot access my account?
  1. Check that you are using the Google account you registered with.
  2. Read the message on the log-in page. It tells you if the account is pending, rejected or suspended.
  3. Follow the login troubleshooting guide.
  4. If nothing works, contact support using the temporary channels.
How do I switch themes?

Open Settings → Theme and choose Dark, Light or AMOLED. Your choice is remembered on this device.

Dashboard & academic tools

How does the dashboard work?

The Dashboard summarizes your active semester: semester progress, your number of subjects, GWA, attendance and tasks, plus today's schedule, upcoming tasks, an attendance breakdown and grade performance. It is built from the information you enter elsewhere in the planner, so it fills up as you add subjects, tasks, attendance and grades.

How do I manage my tasks and deadlines?

Open Tasks and choose Add Task. The counters at the top show how many tasks are active, done and overdue. Tasks that are due soon also appear as reminders on the Notifications page and in the dashboard's upcoming tasks.

How do I create and organize notes?

Open Notes and choose Add Note. You can:

  • Pick a category, and optionally link a subject or task.
  • Add tags by typing one and pressing Enter.
  • Format text with the toolbar (bold, italic, headings, lists, checklists, quotes, code and links) or start from a template.
  • Pin or favorite a note so it is easy to find.
  • Search your notes and export a note as a PDF.
How do I manage my class schedule?

Add your subjects and class times in Schedule for the active semester. The Calendar then shows classes, tasks and events together. To start a new semester or switch between semesters, use Settings → Academic Semesters. Each semester keeps its own subjects, attendance, grades, tasks and notes.

How do I record and track my grades?

Open Grades. For each subject, set up grading components (for example quizzes or exams) with their weights, then add your assessments with a score and the total number of items. The page calculates the subject's standing and your GWA as you enter scores. These figures are estimates based on what you enter and are not official records.

How is my GWA calculated?
  1. Each assessment becomes a percentage: score ÷ total items.
  2. A component's percentage is the average of its assessments.
  3. A subject's final percentage combines its components using their weights. Components with no scores yet are left out until you add some.
  4. That percentage is converted to a grade point on the 1.00 to 5.00 scale. For example, 96.50–100 is 1.00, 80.50–84.49 is 2.00, 64.50–68.49 is 3.00, and anything below 64.50 is 5.00.
  5. Your overall GWA is the average of your graded subjects' grade points, weighted by each subject's units.
How do I manage attendance?

In Attendance, log each class as Present, Late, Excused, Absent or No Classes. Your attendance rate is (Present + Late + Excused) ÷ all records except No Classes. The page also tracks your absences against a limit for each subject, and your consecutive absences.

How do I access the curriculum?

Open Curriculum to view the curriculum of your program as a PDF. If it says the curriculum is not available yet, it has not been added for your program.

AI Study Assistant

What is the AI Study Assistant?

A chat assistant for study help. Ask it about your lessons, ask for explanations, or attach a file for it to review. It uses an AI model through the planner's backend, so it needs an internet connection and a signed-in session.

How do I start a new conversation?

Open the AI Study Assistant and select New chat, then type your question in the message box.

How do I access previous conversations?

Use the conversation menu at the top of the AI Study Assistant. It lists your most recent conversations (up to 50) and has a search box. Open one to continue it, or use the menu on a conversation to rename or delete it. Conversations are saved to your account.

Can I upload files for AI assistance?

Yes. Select the paperclip to attach a PDF, JPG or PNG file of up to 8 MB. The AI Quiz Maker accepts the same file types.

Why might AI-generated responses be inaccurate?

AI models generate answers from patterns, not from a verified source, so they can be wrong, incomplete or out of date, and they can sound confident when they are mistaken. Always check important answers against your class materials, textbooks and instructors.

Are there limitations to AI-generated content?
  • Messages can be up to 6,000 characters.
  • Attachments must be PDF, JPG or PNG files of 8 MB or less.
  • Responses can take several seconds, and a request that takes too long shows an error you can retry.
  • The AI is a study aid. It is not a substitute for your instructors or for official course materials.

Data & privacy

Where is my information stored?

When you are signed in, your planner data (semesters, subjects, schedule, tasks, calendar events, notes, attendance and grades) is saved to the planner's online database, and a copy is kept in your browser so pages load quickly. Your AI conversations and saved quizzes are saved to your account as well. Your display settings, such as theme, stay on the device.

Can other students see my personal records?

No. Inside the planner, your records belong to your account and other students cannot open them. Administrators manage account approvals and handle the reports and messages you send them. Do not share your account with anyone.

What happens to my data when I sign out?

The planner first waits for any pending changes to finish syncing. It then signs you out and clears your planner data from that browser. Your data stays saved in your account and loads again the next time you sign in. Your display settings are kept.

How do I report a potential security or privacy issue?

Please do not post it publicly. Use Report a problem inside the app (choose Technical Problem) or the temporary support email once it is available. Describe what you noticed, and never include passwords or sign-in tokens.

Troubleshooting

Why is the application not loading?

Usually a connection problem, an outdated browser or a content blocker. Follow the "App not loading" guide.

Why are my changes not being saved?

Changes are saved to your account over the internet. If you are offline or the connection drops, they may not sync. Follow the "Data not saving or syncing" guide.

What should I do if a page displays an error?

Refresh the page first (Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows, Cmd+Shift+R on Mac). If the error returns, sign out and back in. If it still appears, send a report with the page name, what you were doing, and a screenshot. See Report a bug.

Why is the application not working properly on mobile?

The planner is built to work on phones. Update your browser, refresh the page, and turn off any content blocker for the site. If something still looks wrong, report it with your phone model and browser. See the browser compatibility guide.

What should I do if the PWA is not installing?

Not every browser supports installing web apps. Follow the "App installation" guide.

Troubleshooting guides

Step-by-step fixes for the most common problems. Try them in order.

App not loading

The page stays blank, keeps spinning, or looks unstyled.

  1. Check that your internet connection works. The planner needs it to load your data.
  2. Refresh the page. For a full refresh, press Ctrl+Shift+R (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac).
  3. Try a private or incognito window. If it works there, a browser extension (such as an ad or script blocker) may be blocking the app.
  4. Update your browser to the latest version.
  5. As a last step, clear this site's data in your browser settings and sign in again. Before you do, check Settings → Sync Status for errors, because changes that have not synced yet could be lost.
Login and authentication problems

You cannot sign in, or you are sent back to the log-in page.

  1. Use the Google account registered under lastname.firstname.middlename@gmail.com. Other accounts are not accepted.
  2. If Google offers several accounts, pick the correct one in the account chooser.
  3. Read the message shown on the log-in page. It tells you if the account is awaiting approval, was not approved, or is suspended.
  4. If your account is pending, use Check status on the pending page.
  5. Try a private window, or clear this site's cookies and cache, and sign in again.
  6. Still stuck? Contact support through the temporary channels.
Data not saving or syncing

Your changes disappear after a refresh, or you see a "could not sync" message.

  1. Check your internet connection.
  2. Open Settings → Sync Status and select Refresh & Retry.
  3. Wait a few seconds after making a change before you close the tab or sign out, so it has time to sync.
  4. Check that you are viewing the right semester. Each semester has its own data.
  5. Create a backup from Settings → Data & Storage → Export All Data (JSON) before clearing browser data or using Reset All Data.
AI Study Assistant errors

The assistant shows an error, does not reply, or rejects your file.

  1. Session expired: sign out and sign in again.
  2. Taking too long: wait a moment and send it again, or shorten your message (up to 6,000 characters).
  3. File problem: attach a PDF, JPG or PNG that is 8 MB or smaller.
  4. Connection problem: check that you are online, then retry.
  5. If the same error keeps appearing, report it with the exact message and the time it happened.
App installation (PWA) issues

You cannot find an install option for the planner.

  1. Open Settings → App Installation. It tells you whether your device can install the app right now, or whether it is already installed.
  2. On Chrome, Edge or Brave, use the Install button there, or the install icon in the address bar.
  3. On iPhone or iPad, open the planner in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Some browsers cannot install web apps. You can keep using the planner in the browser.
Browser compatibility problems

Parts of the page look broken or buttons do nothing.

  1. Use an up-to-date version of Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox or Safari.
  2. Make sure JavaScript is on and site data (cookies and local storage) is not blocked for this site.
  3. Turn off content or script blockers for the planner, since the app loads some files from public CDNs.
  4. Avoid private browsing for regular use, because it clears site data when the window closes.
  5. Do a full refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R), then try again.

Feedback & bug reporting

Bug report

Something is broken or behaves differently from what you expect, for example a button that does nothing or a wrong number.

Feature request

An idea for something new or a way to improve something that already works.

What to include

  • What you were trying to do, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The page you were on and the steps to repeat the problem.
  • Your device, browser and theme (Light, Dark or AMOLED).
  • A screenshot, with any personal details hidden.
  • The exact text of any error message.

Keep it safe. Never share passwords, sign-in or authentication tokens, or sensitive personal information in a public report. For anything private, use Report a problem inside the app instead of GitHub.
