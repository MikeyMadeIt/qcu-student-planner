    Step 3 of 3: Terms and Privacy

    Terms & Privacy

    Please read and accept the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Notice before submitting your registration.

    Terms and Conditions

    1. Draft notice

    These Terms and Conditions are a draft prepared for the QCU Student Planner. They must be reviewed and approved by the responsible university offices before the application is deployed to students.

    2. Purpose of the application

    The planner helps students organize class schedules, tasks, grades, attendance records and notes. It is a personal productivity tool and is not an official record of the university.

    3. Acceptable use

    You agree to use the planner only for lawful, personal academic purposes. You must not attempt to access another person's account or records, interfere with the operation of the service, or submit false or misleading information.

    4. Account responsibility

    You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for activity that takes place under your account. Choose a password that is not used on other services, and notify an administrator if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

    5. Academic information

    Schedules, grades, attendance and notes that you enter remain your own responsibility. You should check them against official university records, because the planner may contain errors or omissions that you introduced or that result from a software fault.

    6. Account approval

    New accounts are not active until an administrator has reviewed the program, campus and year level you submitted. An account may be declined, suspended or removed if the information cannot be verified or if these terms are not followed.

    7. Changes to these terms

    These terms may be revised before and after launch. Continued use of the planner after a revision means you accept the updated terms.

    Privacy Notice

    1. Draft notice

    This Privacy Notice is a draft that describes the intended handling of information. It has not been reviewed by the university's data protection officer and must be confirmed before production deployment.

    2. Information the system is intended to collect

    To create an account the system collects your full name, personal email address, a password, your academic program, campus, year level and section. Your student number is not collected during registration.

    3. Why the information is collected

    Your email address and password identify your account. Your name, program, campus, year level and section help administrators tell student accounts apart and verify that each account belongs to an enrolled student before access is granted.

    4. Handling and access

    Registration details are intended to be visible only to authorized administrators who review accounts, and to you. They are not intended to be sold or used for advertising. The exact storage, retention period and security safeguards will be described here once they are decided and verified.

    5. Your choices

    You may ask to view, correct or delete the information tied to your account. The procedure and the contact details for these requests will be published by the university before launch.

    6. Questions

    Until the final contact details are published, direct questions about this notice to the administrators responsible for the QCU Student Planner.
