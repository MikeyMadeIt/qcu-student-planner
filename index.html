Features

Everything for your semester, in one planner

These tools are available now, right after you sign in.

  • Dashboard

    Semester progress, GWA, attendance, tasks and today's schedule on one screen.

  • Subjects and schedule

    Manage your subjects and view classes by day, as a list, or on a timetable.

  • Tasks and assignments

    Track deadlines in list or board view, filter and group tasks, and add checklists.

  • Calendar

    See classes, tasks, events and university dates by month, week or day.

  • Attendance

    Log Present, Late, Excused or Absent for each class and watch your absence limit.

  • Grades and GWA

    Enter grade components per subject and get your GWA on the 1.00 to 5.00 scale.

  • Notes

    Write rich-text notes with checklists, mark favorites, and link them to a subject or task.

  • Curriculum

    Open program curriculum PDFs with page navigation, zoom and rotation.

  • AI study tools

    Generate quizzes and flashcards from a topic, notes or an attached file, or ask the study assistant.

Also included: Light, Dark and AMOLED themes, notifications and inbox, and installation as an app on your device.

About

About QCU Student Planner

QCU Student Planner puts a student's academic life in one place. Instead of switching between a timetable, a to-do list, a grade spreadsheet and loose notes, you manage them together and see how they connect.

It was built by IM101 Group 5, student developers from SBCS 2A at Quezon City University, for academic organization and study support.

  • See your week

    Your schedule, calendar and dashboard show what is coming up before it arrives.

  • Stay on top of deadlines

    Tasks with due dates and checklists, plus reminders you can switch on in Settings.

  • Know where you stand

    Grades roll up into your GWA, and attendance shows how close you are to the absence limit.

  • Study with help

    Turn your notes into practice quizzes and flashcards. Always check AI output yourself.
